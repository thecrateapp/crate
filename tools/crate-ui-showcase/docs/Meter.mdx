<DocHeading as="h2">Contract</DocHeading>

- A readonly quantity against a known positive range, used in Listen and Admin.
- The caption uses MonoLabel; the formatted reading is a single, complete line. Its column
  grows with units and separators instead of wrapping inside a fixed five-character width.
  The track consumes the remaining width. Below320px available width, the component places its
  caption above the track and reading; equivalent adjacent meters share that composition,
  including longer localized captions. In the horizontal layout the rail retains at least64px.
- A known zero is distinct from missing data. Missing or invalid ranges expose a named group
  instead of a false meter or percentage. Supply localized label/value text from the app.
- The rail is a fixed one-pixel shared recipe. Finite positive ranges expose `role=meter`;
  both ARIA value and fill clamp to0..max. Null/non-finite readings display n/a as a named group.
  A known value with an invalid/zero maximum retains its formatted reading without meter semantics.
- `valueRef` targets the fill span (not the reading): imperative owners may set `--v`, while
  the next value prop refreshes it. Optional `limit` marks a bounded ratio of max; `over` adds
  textual warning context as well as color. Preserve callback/object ref cleanup.
- Keep native range semantics, bounded fill, optional warning/limit and callback/object refs.
  No caller typography, width or geometry repairs.
