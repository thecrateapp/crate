<DocHeading as="h2">Contract</DocHeading>

- Receives an ISO local-calendar `YYYY-MM-DD` date and a locale from its caller. It creates
  the date at local noon, so a calendar day is not interpreted as a UTC midnight.
- A valid date renders a semantic `time` with the exact `dateTime` and a localized full-date
  accessible name. Month/weekday use actual MonoLabel; the day retains its existing display role.
- Missing, malformed and impossible dates render nothing, rather than a fake day or a Date error.
  A caller supplies any desired unknown-date copy outside the stamp.
- The stamp reserves56px; UpcomingEventRow owns its separate date lane. No dates are fetched
  or inferred by this component. The example demonstrates a leap day and Spanish formatting.
