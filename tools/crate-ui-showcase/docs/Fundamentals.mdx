## Link feedback — 1BD amendment

Links in both apps use the shared `link-meta` / `link-accent` recipes. Hover changes foreground
color; accent links use the high accent. Diego removed all animated link beams, their generated
underline layers and their keyframes. Normal and reduced motion share the same feedback.
Native keyboard focus stays visible; truncated `link-inset` anchors retain an inset outline
and static underline on focus. This concerns link feedback; tab indicators and player transport
keep their independent interaction contracts. See the live Foundations/Links story.

Linked StatNumber captions use the same high-accent foreground on hover and keyboard focus,
with no beam or background. This behavior belongs to StatNumber itself. The numeral and unit
keep their roles, and readonly metric captions remain subtle gray. See StatNumber/Linked.

The same foreground rule applies to enabled links containing MonoLabel: hover and visible
keyboard focus use high accent. Informational/disabled labels keep their tone. Context-menu
options containing an actual MonoLabel share this feedback and keep a transparent hover/focus
background. Their resting state colors, target sizes and focus outline remain unchanged.
The menu rule is scoped to actual MonoLabel options; body-text menu items keep their separate
interaction recipe. Disabled options retain their resting feedback.

Meter reserves its full formatted reading on one line (minimum5ch), with a flexible track; units and separators never wrap and displace adjacent instrument captions. See Components/Data Display/Meter.

Meter owns its narrow layout: below320px available width, captions move above the rail and single-line reading. This keeps localized neighboring instruments aligned without caller overrides.

Loading reserves neutral static blocks for known card/media/table layouts. Generic sections use
a compact caption/hairline, never table-shaped dash readings. Route delay and motion gates stay
unchanged. See the four Feedback skeleton entries and LoadingState for actual compositions.

Search loading is an immediate section state: `fullWidth` fills its available panel content width. CatalogSearch names a single requested entity type; global/mixed searches say Finding your music... in the active locale. The 200ms route mark keeps its separate fixed geometry.

The Stats explorer recipe (`stats-explorer-*`) is a hand-built Listen instrument: an overview
strip with a draggable window, a detail chart whose scale column sizes to its longest localized
label, and an "In this stretch" panel that moves below the chart in containers under 50rem. Its
only motion is the one-time opening from today backwards (ease-out-expo); reduced motion renders
the final state and nothing moves at rest. Its actual view is Listen's StatsExplorerView.

Explorer edge handles reach outward only (44px in total on coarse pointers), so even a 16px
window keeps its body draggable; the press position, not the hit element, chooses move or
resize. The strip keeps `touch-action: none` above app-level coarse `[tabindex]` rules, or the
browser cancels the drag to pan. While a new stretch loads, the previous server blocks stay
dimmed in place; a failed read says so instead of showing an empty stretch.

The explorer replaces Listen's old signal tape on the Stats page; the tape's cursor, tooltip, axis
and legend recipes (`stats-signal-tape`, `-cursor`, `-tip`, `-axis`, `-legend-*`) are gone. Only the
bar and mood-line recipes that Home's mini tape still draws remain (`stats-signal-svg`, `-bar`,
`-mood`), plus the loading skeleton. The Listen/Stats review hosts the explorer's real view and
panel with local fixtures and no network.

The listening heatmap band (`stats-heatmap-*`) replaces Listen's old heatmap card. It is an HTML
`role="grid"` of 168 hours where each `role="row"` is its own grid: an aria-hidden weekday column
plus `repeat(24, minmax(0, 1fr))`, with no subgrid or `display: contents`. A cell's one shared
style carries `--stats-level` and `--stats-delay`. The level is a `::before` opacity of
`0.05 + level * 0.95` over `accent-action-hi`, with glow only from level 0.6. Night hours,
the peak and the current hour are attributes, not inline styles. Night cells get the quiet-subtle
surface and the night hours get a hairline on the hour axis. The peak and now outlines sit on
`::after` (the now outline is dashed), so the focus ring stays free. Rows grow from 18px to 24px
in bands of 40rem or wider. Forced colors show cell borders, the level in `Highlight`, the peak
outline in `Highlight` and the axis rule under night hours only.

The heatmap's only entrance is a diagonal wave on the first real payload. It waits for its
section's reveal, and later payloads transition the level's opacity. Reduced motion renders the
final state. The actual view is Listen's `ListeningHeatmap`. The Listen/Stats review shows it with
the now hour (personal), with its grid hidden below three Crate Pulse listeners (D30), and with
a flat zero-minute grid.

Track metadata follows the row's own container, rather than the viewport. Quality appears at
640px of content width and BPM/key at800px, reserving space for the rank,48px cover and readable
title. Quality, analysis, duration, like and menu keep their fixed slots; missing values do not
collapse a column. Narrow rows retain their standard duration/actions layout. Stats rankings
use the full page width instead of sharing a two-column layout with artist posters. The
Stats row consistency review and the track metadata review exercise the same production pieces
at375/640/760/900/1200px; they do not repair the row with scene CSS.

## Metering and progress reconciliation — October 2026

Meter and ProgressHairline keep one-pixel rails owned by the shared recipe. Meter reports a
known range with bounded fill, a complete Mono reading and optional limit tick; unavailable
measurements are named groups, not invented zero percentages. ProgressHairline uses ratios from
0 to1 and only animates unknown progress while intersecting and motion is allowed. The Radix
Progress facade takes values from0 to its max (100 by default). Their component entries cover
these different contracts; callers must not mix their scales or thicken rails locally.

Typography retains a separate `text-meta` token for compact technical metadata. It does not
replace MonoLabel, informative-badge or filter typography. Compact counts use regular Mono;
Bricolage display numerals remain an explicit StatNumber role rather than a blanket counter font.
The Icons story now renders the actual Solar Tag, GripVertical and ArrowDownToLineBold aliases
inside shared ActionButton controls, with accessible names and the common tooltip/target recipe.

This catalogue reconciliation documents current source. It does not certify all Admin/Listen
consumers or add a new visual approval. The focused progress/upcoming stories reuse actual
components; no source geometry or canonical capture was changed.

Token hygiene (2026-10-26): the nine visualizer ribbon stops the consolidated visualizer no
longer reads were removed from `product.css`; `--visualizer-ribbon-stop-1` remains its active
gradient. The Stats heatmap night band border uses `--border-quiet`, and the reduced-motion tabs
indicator rule keys off `[data-crate-motion="reduced"]` like the other recipes. No visual change.

Overlay sizes (2026-10-26): `2xl` (1024px) and `3xl` (1280px) join the overlay width scale for
dense Admin dialogs such as artist repair and user detail.

Toolbox icon (2026-10-26): the official Solar Toolbox Outline is inlined like Bot, since it was
added upstream after the installed 2.1.1. Admin uses it for the artist Repair action instead of
the Tuning glyph, which reads as an equalizer.

Icon library (2026-10-26): the catalogue moved from `@solar-icons/react-perf` 2.1.1, which stopped
receiving new glyphs, to `@solar-icons/react` 2.3.3 with per-icon imports. Public names and
`CRATE_ICON_SIZE` are unchanged; Bot and Toolbox now come from the package instead of inlined SVG.
Icons draw at 1em and take their size through font-size, so caller `size-*` classes still win.
Six glyphs differ from the previous release: Share, ArchiveDown (Bold), VolumeCross,
HamburgerMenu, Ticket and MagicStick (now Solar Magic Wand).

Empty states (2026-10-26): `crate-empty-state` centres its content (items and text) with 24px inline
padding inside any container, and keeps its 32px block padding and 160px minimum. The recipe is
unlayered, so caller `py-*`, `min-h-*` or alignment utilities have no effect.

Page titles (2026-10-26): `crate-page-title` is the single 800 page-title recipe (Bricolage 800,
`clamp(2.25rem,4vw,3.75rem)`, display leading, -0.045em, balanced). `data-size="md"` fixes it at
2.25rem. It declares `font-family: var(--font-display)`, so the Admin font-role policy recognises it as the
display role. PageHeader and the Admin page titles use it instead of hand-written utility copies.

Text panel tabs scroll without drawing a scrollbar when they overflow, and tighten their gap to 16px at 480px and below so four tabs fit a phone.

`crate-touch-target` gives a compact control an invisible coarse-pointer hit area of at least 44px around its visible box, so touch reach does not change the control's size.
