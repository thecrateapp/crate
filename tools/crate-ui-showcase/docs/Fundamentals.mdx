## Link feedback — 1BD amendment

Links in both apps use the shared `link-meta` / `link-accent` recipes. Hover changes foreground
color; accent links use the high accent. Diego removed all animated link beams, their generated
underline layers and their keyframes. Normal and reduced motion share the same feedback.
Native keyboard focus stays visible; truncated `link-inset` anchors retain an inset outline
and static underline on focus. This concerns link feedback; tab indicators and player transport
keep their independent interaction contracts. See the live Foundations/Links story.

Linked StatNumber captions use the same high-accent foreground on hover and keyboard focus,
with no beam or background. This behavior belongs to StatNumber itself. The numeral and unit
keep their roles, and readonly metric captions remain subtle gray. See StatNumber/Linked.

The same foreground rule applies to enabled links containing MonoLabel: hover and visible
keyboard focus use high accent. Informational/disabled labels keep their tone. Context-menu
options containing an actual MonoLabel share this feedback and keep a transparent hover/focus
background. Their resting state colors, target sizes and focus outline remain unchanged.
The menu rule is scoped to actual MonoLabel options; body-text menu items keep their separate
interaction recipe. Disabled options retain their resting feedback.

Meter reserves its full formatted reading on one line (minimum5ch), with a flexible track; units and separators never wrap and displace adjacent instrument captions. See Components/Data Display/Meter.

Meter owns its narrow layout: below320px available width, captions move above the rail and single-line reading. This keeps localized neighboring instruments aligned without caller overrides.

Loading reserves neutral static blocks for known card/media/table layouts. Generic sections use
a compact caption/hairline, never table-shaped dash readings. Route delay and motion gates stay
unchanged. See the four Feedback skeleton entries and LoadingState for actual compositions.

Search loading is an immediate section state: `fullWidth` fills its available panel content width. CatalogSearch names a single requested entity type; global/mixed searches say Finding your music... in the active locale. The 200ms route mark keeps its separate fixed geometry.

The Stats explorer recipe (`stats-explorer-*`) is a hand-built Listen instrument: an overview
strip with a draggable window, a detail chart whose scale column sizes to its longest localized
label, and an "In this stretch" panel that moves below the chart in containers under 50rem. Its
only motion is the one-time opening from today backwards (ease-out-expo); reduced motion renders
the final state and nothing moves at rest. Its actual view is Listen's StatsExplorerView.

Explorer edge handles reach outward only (44px in total on coarse pointers), so even a 16px
window keeps its body draggable; the press position, not the hit element, chooses move or
resize. The strip keeps `touch-action: none` above app-level coarse `[tabindex]` rules, or the
browser cancels the drag to pan. While a new stretch loads, the previous server blocks stay
dimmed in place; a failed read says so instead of showing an empty stretch.

The explorer replaces Listen's old signal tape on the Stats page; the tape's cursor, tooltip, axis
and legend recipes (`stats-signal-tape`, `-cursor`, `-tip`, `-axis`, `-legend-*`) are gone. Only the
bar and mood-line recipes that Home's mini tape still draws remain (`stats-signal-svg`, `-bar`,
`-mood`), plus the loading skeleton. The Listen/Stats review hosts the explorer's real view and
panel with local fixtures and no network.
