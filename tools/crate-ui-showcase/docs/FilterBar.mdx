<DocHeading as="h2">Contract</DocHeading>

Composes leading actions, growing search, stable-width sort, trailing actions and a separate
wrapping chip row. Empty rows are omitted. Group names are optional and supplied by the app.
`align="center"` preserves unlabelled control rows; `align="end"` aligns controls with stacked
FormField captions along their bottom edges. FormField owns the8px gap and its label; FilterBar
does not resize controls or compensate for labels with margins. Controls wrap at narrow widths.
The shared Select width does not depend on its selected option. Listen liked tracks opts into
bottom alignment; other existing Admin/Listen consumers retain center alignment by default.

The approved mockup and Diego's subsequent amendments remain the visual reference. This entry
does not certify all legacy consumers or native/AT/phase QA.
