## Shared focus lifecycle · 24 October review

`useModalFocus` is the same lifecycle used by AppModal and Listen's specialized player surfaces. It enters the active panel, wraps Tab only in the top layer, respects a portalled child's Escape/Tab ownership and restores the opening trigger. A specialized fullscreen surface can additionally isolate its background branches without replacing its layout or transition. A resolver supports a launcher that is recreated after closing; a non-modal workspace may keep the player dock accessible while open. Closed animated player surfaces remain mounted but are inert and hidden from assistive technology during their exit.

The Base story's nested dialog and portalled Select/Tooltip exercise the unchanged facade. The shared focus tests cover the specialized scope with a nested AppModal and focus return. Listen's player tests cover prepared/closed exclusion, fullscreen naming and isolation, boundary Tab wrapping, the recreated launcher and nested Escape priority. Real browser/native keyboard and touch animation checks remain consumer responsibilities; no playback is needed for those focus checks.
