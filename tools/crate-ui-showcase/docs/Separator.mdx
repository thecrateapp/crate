One-pixel horizontal or vertical rule, decorative by default with explicit semantic mode.

Horizontal:1px high and full width. Vertical:1px wide and fills an explicitly sized parent. Subtle-border color distinguishes sibling regions; InstrumentPanel owns its quieter top rule. Decorative=true avoids screen-reader noise; decorative=false exposes the native separator role and vertical orientation when needed. Native ref and ARIA pass through. No focus target or click behavior.

The approved persistent mockup and confirmed amendments are the reference. Scene layout never repairs component internals.
