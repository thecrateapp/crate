# Crate 3.0.2

Crate 3.0.2 makes generated queues and radio behave like a good listening session: Infinite
Playback keeps going across albums and playlists, radio stops repeating what you just heard,
Music Paths stay coherent and finite, and the next track is chosen by how it enters the one
that is playing, not only by genre and similarity. It includes two database migrations.

## Listening

- **The next track enters smoothly.** Radio and Music Paths now judge the seam between the
  playing track and each candidate from the Smart Mix profile: the loudness of the last and
  first five seconds, brightness, silence between tracks, tempo and key. Electronic sets lean on
  tempo and key so tracks can be beatmatched; rock and other guitar music lean on a soft entry,
  so a loud cold open no longer follows a quiet ending. On two captured rock seeds the average
  edge level jump fell from 7.7 to 2.4 dB and from 3.5 to 2.2 dB, with no loss of genre
  coherence or artist variety. `CRATE_TRANSITION_FIT_ENABLED=false` restores the previous
  ranking.
- **Radio rotates.** Seeded radio prefers coherent songs you have not heard in the last seven
  days, and a song delivered in the session, even one skipped after a few seconds, does not come
  back while a fresh alternative exists. `CRATE_RADIO_ROTATION_ENABLED=false` restores the
  previous behaviour.
- **Infinite Playback keeps refilling** albums and playlists across web, desktop, Android and
  Cast, keeps the original seed and liked tracks, and shows a translated notice instead of an
  endless spinner when no more tracks can be found. Selecting a track after that notice resumes
  refilling without skipping it.
- **Android releases audio focus** when continuation stops, keeps its state when you return to
  the app, and keeps the notification and lock-screen controls after a finite queue ends.
- **Cast** distinguishes the gap between tracks from a pause and recovers queues after lost
  acknowledgements.
- **Music Paths** avoid recently heard songs, keep the principal artist from repeating, show
  partial journeys honestly, and respond faster.

## VirtualDJ

- The compatible-tracks folder ranks by the same transition fit. `context=beatmatch` or
  `context=smooth` forces either style; `auto` derives it from genre and rhythm evidence. The
  response adds breakdown fields without changing existing ones; the planner contract stays
  `smart-mix-v2`.

## Upgrade notes

- **Migration 139** restores the Bliss cosine index concurrently on the first API start. On a
  large library it can take a minute; it does nothing if the index already exists.
- **Migration 140** stores Music Path generation metadata.
- Native continuation rejects the unshipped numeric `revision` field; released apps do not send
  it.

  `crate-windows.msi`, `crate-linux.AppImage`, `crate-linux.deb` and `crate-linux.rpm`. Each is

  are ad-hoc signed.
- **Android:** `crate.apk` (signed and minified), plus the versioned APK and App Bundle.
