# Crate 3.3.0

Crate 3.3.0 brings Smart Mix 3: a deeper analysis of each track (phrases, voice and beats
from Beat This!), the `smart-mix-v3` planner for clients that ask for it, and analyzer v9,
which also decodes `.m4a` files at their real speed. Smart Mix playlists are separated into
stems on the server for Crate Decks. Admin gets an Errors inbox with AI triage and fixes
launched from Crate. The database goes from migration 144 to 158.

## Smart Mix 3

- **Analysis.** Profiles now carry phrase structure, where the voice is (YAMNet), beats and
  downbeats from Beat This! with madmom's DBN, and harmony and timbre novelty.
- **Analyzer v9.** v8 measures beat stability and confidence on the track's own grid, so
  more tracks get a `full` profile. v9 reads the AAC in `.m4a` as stereo instead of
  decoding it at half speed (twice the duration, half the tempo, no loudness), runs Beat
  This! on `.m4a` too, and keeps the bars of tracks whose model downbeats drift half a beat
  in some sections.
- **Model beats stay.** A reanalysis with the analyzer's own beat tracker no longer
  replaces a profile of the same version whose beats came from Beat This!.
- **Planner v3.** `smart-mix-v3` plans transitions on phrases, voice and the beat grid.
  The API still answers `smart-mix-v2` unless a client asks for v3.
- **Backfill.** Eight analyses run at once from 01:00 to 08:00 Europe/Madrid and four by
  day. A claim pass reads a stored ranking instead of ranking the whole library.
- **AI refinement** of transitions (`smart_mix.transition.refine`) is in, and stays off
  after its evaluation.

## Smart Mix playlists and stems

- **Smart Mix playlists.** An owner turns Smart Mix on for a playlist from its menu in
  Listen, and Listen marks those playlists. Each instance caps the tracks per playlist
  (300 by default) and, if it wants, per user.
- **Stem separation.** The analysis worker separates the tracks of Smart Mix playlists
  into drums, bass, other and vocals with `htdemucs` on ONNX Runtime (CPU), the
  playlist's tracks first. It keeps each track's stem levels and drum beats, and the
  stem audio under a 50 GiB quota on the music volume.
- **Admin.** A track's audio profile shows its stems, and Analysis gets a Smart Mix
  playlists panel.
- **Crate Decks.** Decks reads and uploads stem analyses, downloads the stem audio, and
  gets each `smart-mix-v3` plan's candidate menu with the limits it refines within. The
  planner scores that menu with the stems (no two voices or basslines at once) only for
  Crate Decks: Listen, Android and VirtualDJ get the same plans as without stems.

## Errors

- **Errors inbox.** Sentry issues are triaged and investigated with AI on the `ai`
  lanes. Automatic triage is off by default; **Triage backlog** and **Investigate** run
  on demand. Issue releases are filled in from Sentry.
- **Fixes from Crate.** **Launch fix** starts the autofix workflow for one issue, and the
  issue's Fix panel follows its run and pull request: checks, mergeability and state.
- **Releases in Sentry.** Images report their commits to Sentry, and `make deploy` records
  each deploy.
- **Critical errors alert.** The Operator gets an `errors-critical` rule, shipped
  disabled.

## Fixes

- **Album page.** The album page on mobile no longer crashes with "Maximum update depth
  exceeded".
- **AI providers.** OpenAI-compatible endpoints can be connected, and changing an Ollama
  connection's endpoint drops its stored key.
- **Prereleases.** Prereleases are reconciled with the full album release.

## Upgrading

- Migrations 145 to 158 run on start. They add tables and columns, and rebuild the AI
  actions' and providers' constraints.
- Profiles of earlier analyzer versions stay in use until a Smart Mix backfill campaign
  from Admin recomputes them with v9. Libraries with `.m4a` files need one: their earlier
  profiles read the audio at half speed.
- **Stems directory.** The stem audio must live on the music's filesystem, owned by
  `PUID:PGID`. `docker-compose.yaml` mounts `STEMS_DIR`, or `${MEDIA_DIR}/smart-mix-stems`
  when it is not set. `CRATE_STEMS_THREADS` (4 by default) sets the separation's threads.
  The home profile does not mount a stems directory yet: Smart Mix playlists can be turned
  on there, but their tracks are not separated.
