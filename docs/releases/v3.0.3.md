# Crate 3.0.3

Crate 3.0.3 fixes problems found while listening to 3.0.2: Music Paths that let one
artist fill a dozen steps, a transition rule that judged a fade-out as a loud jump, and
Android playback that ignored Play once a queue had ended. It needs no database
migration.

Crate is published under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0).

## Fixes

- **Music Paths no longer chain one artist through guest credits.** A credit such as
  "Flying Lotus, Thom Yorke" or "Erik Urano, Flat Erik, …" now belongs to its album
  owner unless the full name is a library artist, so bands with commas or ampersands in
  their names stay whole. A path from The Clash to Erik Urano that played twelve Flying
  Lotus tracks in a row now moves through punk, post-punk, electronic and grime with no
  artist repeated back to back.
- **Music Paths take at most two tracks from one album**, never two in a row. When a
  scene runs out of artists, a step borrows from the previous scene instead of leaving
  the path short.
- **Smoother entries after a fade-out.** Radio and Music Paths compare the next track
  with the body loudness of the outgoing one when it fades out, instead of with the
  fade itself. On a live Citizen radio replay the average perceived level jump fell from
  6.7 dB in 3.0.2 to 3.7 dB, with the same genre coherence. Music Paths without
  waypoints now use this transition fit too.
- **Android plays an ended track again.** Pressing Play on the finished current track,
  in the list, the mini player, the lock screen or a headset, restarts it from the
  beginning.

## Downloads

- **Server:** `curl -fsSL https://cratemusic.app/install.sh | bash` for new home installs,
  or `crate install --dir <install folder>` to upgrade one. Binaries for Linux and macOS
  on amd64 and arm64 are attached, with `crate-checksums.txt`.
- **Desktop:** `Crate-macos-arm64.app.zip`, `Crate-macos-intel.app.zip`,
  `crate-windows.exe`, `crate-windows.msi`, `crate-linux.AppImage`, `crate-linux.deb` and
  `crate-linux.rpm`. Each is also at
  `https://github.com/thecrateapp/crate/releases/latest/download/<file>`. macOS builds
  are ad-hoc signed.
- **Android:** `crate.apk` (signed and minified), plus the versioned APK and App Bundle.
